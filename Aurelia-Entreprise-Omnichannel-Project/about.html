ABOUT ME

Business writing, transformation, development and data.

I am an author, speaker, entrepreneur, executive coach, strategic consultant and data science graduate. My work is centred on the relationship between how organisations think, how they develop, how they transform and how they create value.

My interests include business writing, strategy, organisational development, transformation and growth. I use writing and research to examine business problems at the level of systems, decisions, incentives, behaviour and execution rather than treating performance as a collection of isolated symptoms.

Data science extends that way of working. I use data to interrogate business questions, explore patterns, engineer useful variables and build models where modelling is appropriate. The objective is not to make a business question “technical”; it is to make the evidence stronger, more transparent and more useful for decision-making.

This portfolio therefore brings together two forms of work that are often separated: analytical depth and business interpretation. The notebooks show the technical process. The visual evidence shows what was found. The business-problem layer explains why the analysis matters.
