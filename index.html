FLAGSHIP CASE

Customer retention & churn.

A complete business-to-analysis workflow using customer, order, payment, return, marketing and support data. The case is intentionally transparent about what the data can and cannot support.

01 · PROBLEM

Retention decisions begin before the intervention.

How can customer behaviour be structured into evidence that helps a business investigate inactivity and retention risk?

02 · EVIDENCE

10,211 customers modelled.

The observed inactive rate is 12.74%. The portfolio documents the target definition and its retrospective limitation.

03 · DECISION

Use the evidence with the right boundary.

The analysis supports investigation and prioritisation; it is not presented as a production forecasting system without an event-timestamped label.
