Scientific methodology

A repeatable analytical workflow.

Zenith uses a consistent project architecture so that business reasoning, data quality, modelling and interpretation remain traceable.

The portfolio distinguishes analytical evidence from business interpretation and records limitations rather than presenting model outputs as business truth.

Workflow

  1. Business Problem — define the decision and analytical question.
  2. Data Audit — inspect structure, types, missingness and quality.
  3. Data Cleaning — make transformations explicit and reproducible.
  4. Data Profiling — understand distributions and data behaviour.
  5. Feature Engineering — create analytical variables linked to the business problem.
  6. Business EDA — investigate patterns, segments and relationships.
  7. Feature Engineering EDA — validate derived features before modelling.
  8. Model — apply an appropriate analytical or machine-learning method.
  9. Business Conclusions — translate evidence into decision-relevant interpretation and document limitations.

Analytical domains

Customer analytics · customer lifetime value · marketing conversion · sales and order economics · inventory demand · store productivity · product profitability · service satisfaction · returns risk · payment risk · supplier reliability · NLP/customer voice.

Important modelling boundary

Project 11 is explicitly framed as payment-failure risk investigation. The supplied data does not provide a fraud ground-truth label, so the portfolio does not present it as a validated fraud classifier.
